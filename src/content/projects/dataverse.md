DataVerse is a self-service analytics platform concept: upload your data, explore it, query it
in SQL, build dashboards, and ask it questions in plain English — without waiting on a data
analyst for every report.

Right now it's genuinely in the **design phase**, not the build phase. Before writing any
application code, the requirements and design have been documented properly, the way a
Business Analyst would hand a project off to a development team: a Business Requirement
Document (objectives, scope, functional and non-functional requirements), an entity
relationship diagram, a system and deployment architecture, and a REST API surface mapped to
requirement IDs.

Planned stack: React, TypeScript, and Tailwind on the frontend; FastAPI, PostgreSQL, and DuckDB
on the backend; AI-assisted querying via OpenAI/Gemini. A full case study — with real
screenshots and a working app — will replace this note once it's actually built.
